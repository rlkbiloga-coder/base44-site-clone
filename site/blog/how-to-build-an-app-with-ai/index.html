01. Define your app

Start by defining your app's purpose and target audience. Are you building a productivity tool, social media platform, eCommerce app or something entirely different? Think about who will use your app and what problem it solves for them. This clarity will guide every decision you make during development.

Before you write a single prompt, it's worth a quick gut-check on the idea itself. Look at whether people are already complaining about the problem you want to solve (forums, reviews and social posts are gold here), see how existing apps handle it and where they fall short, and confirm there's a real audience who'd use what you're picturing. A few hours of this upfront saves you from building something nobody needs.

Consider these popular AI app building categories:

  • Business and productivity app development

  • Ecommerce and marketplace app development

  • Educational and learning app development

  • Entertainment app development

Here are some good questions to ask before deciding which app you want to build with AI:

  1. What specific problem does the app solve and for who?
  2. What outcome should users achieve in their first 60 seconds? (any longer than this and they may bounce)
  3. Who are the primary personas and edge users?

02. Write AI prompts

The quality of your AI-generated app depends heavily on how well you describe your vision. Effective prompts should be detailed but focused. Instead of saying "build me a social app," try something like:

"Create a fitness tracking app for runners where users can log their daily runs, track distance and pace, share achievements with friends and join local running groups. Include features for setting personal goals and viewing progress over time."

Include specific details about:

  • Core functionality and features

  • User types and permissions

  • Data you need to store

  • How users will interact with the app

  • Any integrations you need

Good prompts save time and produce better results. Base44's AI works best when you provide clear, specific instructions about your app's purpose and functionality. You can also refer to a prompt library to find examples and inspiration for creating effective, high-quality prompts.

Need more inspiration? We've put together a few examples of good prompts to use when creating an app with AI.

Looking to start an eCommerce app to sell online?

“Create an eCommerce app for selling shoes online. Include a product catalog with images, size filters, and price sorting. Add a shopping cart, secure checkout, and order tracking. The design should be clean and minimalist, optimized for mobile, with a light color palette and bold product visuals.”

Why it works? It defines the goal (eCommerce for shoes), features (catalog, filters, checkout), and design direction (clean and mobile-friendly).

How about an app to schedule events?

“Build an event scheduling app that lets users create, edit, and share events. Include a calendar view, RSVP system, and push notifications. Allow syncing with Google Calendar and Outlook. Use a modern UI with color-coded events and an intuitive drag-and-drop interface.”

Why it works? It gives specific functionality (RSVP, sync, notifications) and integration details, guiding the AI toward real-world utility.

When building an app with AI, depending on its functionality and usage you may need to consider all or some of the following:

Data

  • What data do we need (inputs, labels, context), and where does it live?

04. Preview your app

Once your app is generated, spend time exploring what the AI created. Test all the main features, navigate through different screens and check that the user experience makes sense.

Look for:

  • Navigation flow between screens

  • Data entry and display

  • Core functionality

  • Mobile responsiveness

Base44 provides a comprehensive mobile preview mode where you can interact with your app as an end user would, giving you a clear picture of what needs refinement.

Pro tip: Take notes on what works well and what needs adjustment. This preview phase helps you understand your app's current capabilities and plan your next steps.

05. Test your app

Testing goes beyond just clicking around. Try to break your app by entering unusual data, testing edge cases and using it the way real users would. Thorough testing is also a key step in learning how to secure an app, ensuring potential vulnerabilities are identified before launch.

Common areas to focus on include:

  • Form validation and error handling

  • Performance with larger amounts of data 

  • User permissions and security

  • Integration with external services

  • Cross-device compatibility

Document any bugs or improvements you discover during testing. This systematic approach ensures your app is robust and user-friendly before launch. For a full checklist, see how to test an app before launch.

How to fix issues in your AI-generated app

When you're testing and find something isn't working, you don't need to start from scratch. Describe the problem in Base44's AI chat the same way you described what you wanted to build: "Users can't submit the contact form" or "The data table isn't filtering correctly." The AI analyzes your app's structure and suggests a targeted fix.

If the first attempt doesn't resolve it, try giving the AI more context. Instead of explaining what's broken, describe what should happen: "When a user clicks Submit, it should save their details to the database and show a confirmation message." The more specific you are about the intended behavior, the better the AI can diagnose the gap.

For trickier issues, use Discuss mode in the AI chat to talk through the problem before applying any changes. This lets you reason through the fix without spending credits on changes you might need to undo. And if you need to go back, Base44's version history lets you restore a previous working version at any time.

Building with AI is iterative by nature. It's normal to spend time in the test-fix-retest cycle, especially for complex workflows. The key is staying systematic: test one feature at a time, note exactly what breaks, and use the AI chat as your debugging partner.

06. Launch and promote your app

Once your app is tested and refined, it’s time to introduce it to users. A strong launch helps people understand what your app does and why it matters. Start by defining a clear value proposition, then create a simple plan for how you’ll share your app with the right audience.

Here are a few effective ways to promote your AI-built app:

Optimize your app store or landing page

  • Highlight your app’s core features

  • Add keywords users are likely to search for

Encourage early engagement

  • Offer incentives like free trials or limited-time features

  • Add a simple onboarding flow that shows users what to do first

  • Use email or push notifications to guide new users

Track performance and iterate

  • Monitor signups, retention and feedback

  • Improve your onboarding, features and flows based on user behavior

  • Update your app regularly to maintain growth and relevance

Launching is not a one-time action. It’s an ongoing process of improving your product, understanding your users and promoting your app across the channels that matter most to your audience. With a thoughtful launch and a clear promotion strategy, your AI-powered app has a much better chance of gaining traction and growing over time.

The evolution of AI in app development

AI app development has come a long way from its early days. The concept of "vibe coding" took off in early 2025, when Andrej Karpathy coined the term to describe working with AI coding tools in a more natural, conversational way. The seeds were planted earlier, though, with Karpathy noting back in 2023 that "English is the hottest new programming language." Vibe coding represents a shift from traditional structured programming to a more intuitive, conversational approach where developers describe what they want in natural language.

Early pioneers like GitHub Copilot and OpenAI Codex showed the potential of AI-assisted coding, but these tools still required significant programming knowledge. The real breakthrough came with platforms like Base44, which introduced true natural language app generation.

This evolution has fundamentally changed how we think about app creation. Instead of writing complex code, you can now describe your vision and watch as AI transforms your words into functioning software. The impact has been enormous—small businesses, entrepreneurs and creative professionals who previously couldn't afford custom app development now have access to powerful creation tools.

Apps can also be created much faster. And how long it takes to build an app has been reduced dramatically.

What AI handles — and what you'll still need to manage

AI app builders have made app creation dramatically faster, but it helps to go in with realistic expectations. The AI excels at generating initial structure: database relationships, user interfaces, authentication flows, and core workflows. That part is genuinely fast.

Where most builders spend more time is the last stretch: fine-tuning specific behaviors, handling edge cases, and making sure everything works exactly as intended for real users. The AI generates a strong foundation, but refining it to match your precise vision takes iteration. Some workflows need a few rounds of prompting before they behave exactly right, and that's a normal part of the process.

A few things you'll want to handle yourself, regardless of which AI app builder you use. Security settings and data access rules need your review before you go live. You'll want to verify that only the right users can see the right data. Integrations with external services (payment processors, email tools, analytics) typically require you to supply API keys and configure the connection. And your app's content (copy, images, brand details) is yours to add.

The good news is that none of this requires writing code. In Base44, you can handle security, integrations, and design customizations through the same chat interface you used to build the app.
