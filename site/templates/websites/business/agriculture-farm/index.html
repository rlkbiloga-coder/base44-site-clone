Farm website templates that follow the season

A farm website changes with the season, and the template needs to keep up without a redesign each time. These pages carry what a farm actually needs: a produce or box list you update as things come in and go, opening hours and visiting details for people driving out, and an order or CSA sign-up that doesn't need a phone call to sort out. Change the produce list, this week's hours, or the wholesale page yourself – you build a website with AI, so a new season is a sentence, not a redesign.

Frequently asked questions

Farm website templates are ready-made website designs built for farms, farm shops, CSA box schemes, and agritourism operations – a produce list, an order or box sign-up, and an opening-hours or visiting page already in place instead of a static page that never changes with the season. Each one is built to be updated as often as the season does.

A box or order form can sit right on the produce page, and payment runs through Base44 Payments powered by Wix, built in from the Builder plan up (availability depends on country); Stripe works as the alternative, though going live with it needs your own Stripe account, and orders are managed from the Stripe Dashboard rather than inside the template – the Stripe integration guide covers the setup if you go that route.

Describe what's in season and what's sold out, and the list updates – there's no separate system to log into. Some farms keep it loose (“seasonal veg box, contents vary”) and others list each item; either works, and switching between them is just a rewritten page, not a rebuild.

The best farm website template is the one that makes a produce list and an opening-hours page easy to update the same day something changes, with an order or box sign-up that doesn't route through a phone call.

  • A produce list that's easy to edit – updated by describing the change, not a separate system.
  • A visible order or box sign-up – so a customer can commit without calling first.
  • Clear hours and visiting details – including room for one-off closures or seasonal changes.
  • Space for wholesale or trade enquiries – kept apart from retail orders.

Base44's farm templates carry all of that already, with every page – the produce list, the hours, the order form – edited by describing the change as the season turns. If the farm needs more than these cover, the wider business website templates are worth a look.
