One-page website templates that bring your message together

Introduce your business, promote an event, or share a new product on a single page. Browse one-page website templates for company sites, agency services, and product launches. Choose a layout that keeps your most important information together, from a short introduction to the details visitors need before getting in touch.

Create a one-page website with your own images, a clear introduction, and space to showcase what you offer. Describe the design changes you need and arrange the sections so visitors can move naturally from learning about you to taking action. Whether you’re inviting inquiries or collecting signups, keep that next step easy to find.

Frequently asked questions

A one page website template is a complete site that lives on a single scrolling page, which you copy and fill with your own content. Everything a small site needs — what you offer, proof, detail, frequently asked questions and one call to action — is arranged in a deliberate order rather than split across a menu. It is live rather than a mockup, so you clone it, replace the words and publish. Changes are described to the one page website builder instead of edited section by section.

Order matters more when creating a one page website, because the visitor cannot navigate around a weak section:

  • What this is — one sentence, before any scrolling.
  • Who it is for — so the wrong reader leaves quickly, which is fine.
  • Proof — a result, a name, a number, a screenshot.
  • The detail — how it works, in three parts, not ten.
  • The objection — price, effort or risk, answered head on.
  • One action — the same one, repeated, never a second competing button.

Each section that has earned its own audience becomes its own page with its own title and description, and the original page becomes the summary that links to them. Because the content already exists and is already written, the split is mostly rearranging. Most one page sites never reach this point, which is the argument for starting here.

Choose on the single action you want:

  • Launch or waiting list — promise first, email capture repeated.
  • Local service — what, where, price, and a phone number that dials.
  • Event or announcement — date, place, and one button.
  • Link page — compact, no scrolling, built for a bio link.
  • Product or app — screenshots doing the explaining, one download.

Clone the closest one and write your real first sentence into it before judging — on a single page, that sentence is most of the design. If it turns out you need more rooms, the website templates hub has the multi-page starting points.
