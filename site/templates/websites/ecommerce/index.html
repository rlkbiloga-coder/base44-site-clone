Frequently asked questions

An ecommerce website template is a working online store you copy and fill with your own products. It arrives with the machinery a store needs already connected: a product catalog with variants and stock levels, a cart, a checkout that takes card payments, shipping and tax rules, and an order record on the other side. The difference from a design theme is that it already transacts. You clone it, load your catalog, connect your payment account and start selling — then change the layout and the rules by describing them to the AI website builder.

Less than a full platform, and all of this:

  • Products with variants and stock — size, color, and a count that goes down.
  • A checkout that takes cards — and shows the total including delivery.
  • Shipping rules — by weight, price or destination; pick one and be exact.
  • Tax handled correctly — the part that is boring and expensive to get wrong.
  • Order confirmation — to the customer and to whoever packs the box.
  • An order list — sortable, searchable, exportable.

What to postpone: reviews, wishlists, loyalty points and abandoned-cart sequences. They matter at volume and cost you time before it. Features to look for in an AI ecommerce builder covers the comparison in more depth.

Base44 hosts and runs the website for you, so there are no servers to size or scale when a sale brings in more visitors than usual – that side is the platform's job, not yours. What you're responsible for is keeping products and stock accurate, since a busy day makes an out-of-date stock count more visible, not less.

The best ecommerce website template is the one built around how you actually sell, not how many products you happen to have today. Match the layout to your selling pattern first:

  • Few products, high value — long product pages, detail over grid.
  • Many products — filtering and search matter more than the hero.
  • Made to order — you need options and lead times, not stock counts.
  • Subscription — recurring billing changes the checkout, so start there.
  • Physical and digital together — delivery rules differ per item type.

Clone the closest one and put twenty real products in it before judging — a catalog behaves differently from a demo. If you need the operational side too, the ecommerce and retail apps category covers inventory and order management.
