Website templates that start with your goals

Showcase your work, introduce your business, or give your next event a place online. Browse website templates for portfolios, online stores, restaurants, and more. Explore the layouts and choose one that gives visitors a clear way to learn about what you offer and take the next step.

Turn your chosen template into a site that feels like yours with Base44 website builder. Replace the sample text and images, adjust colors and fonts, or add pages by describing what you need. Preview your site, check the details, and publish when you’re ready to share it.

Frequently asked questions

Website templates are ready-to-edit sites with the initial layout, pages, styling and content structure already built. You clone one, replace the content with your own and publish. Base44’s website design templates are live sites rather than static mockups, so you can preview how they look and behave. You can then adjust the site with plain-language prompts through the AI website design generator.

Editing a site takes three steps:

  • Clone the template to get your own editable copy.
  • Replace the content — text, images, sections and colors — by describing what you want.
  • Preview on desktop and mobile, then publish.

You can keep editing after launch, and changes go live when you publish again. These AI prompts for building a website help you describe changes precisely.

The best starting point is the one that matches what your visitors need to do. Consider:

  • Goal — read, buy, book, apply or contact? Each needs a different layout.
  • Page set — does it include the pages you need (about, pricing, blog, contact)?
  • Industry — a restaurant or real-estate design carries the right sections by default.
  • Content volume — pick a layout that suits how much text and imagery you actually have.
  • Features — check for store, booking or form support before cloning.
