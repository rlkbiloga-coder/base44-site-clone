Analytics app templates for the metrics that matter

Understand what’s driving sales, how your campaigns are performing, and where your business needs attention. Start with a business intelligence or analytics app template, then customize it around the questions you want your data to answer.

Make it your own with Base44’s AI app builder. Add your data, adjust the layout, and describe the charts or filters you need. Compare monthly revenue, explore results by product, or track progress toward a goal. Your template is a starting point—you can keep refining your dashboard as your reporting needs change.

Frequently asked questions

Business intelligence and analytics app templates are pre-built apps that put your key numbers in one place — charts, totals, filters and a layout, already assembled. You point one at your data instead of designing the report first. The difference that matters is where the numbers come from: a design template shows sample figures, while a working dashboard reads from a live source, so what you see is true right now.

Wherever you keep it. You can connect a database, pull from a service's API, import a spreadsheet on a schedule, or read from another Base44 app you already run. Most dashboards end up combining two or three sources, which is exactly the job that usually forces people back into a manual monthly export. Browse the available integrations to see what connects directly; anything with an API can usually be reached by describing the connection you need.

Fewer numbers than most dashboards carry, chosen more carefully:

  • A small set of decisions — every chart should change something someone does.
  • Comparison — a figure without last period next to it means nothing.
  • Filters that matter — by team, region, product or date range.
  • Drill-down — the ability to ask why a number moved.
  • A clear as-of time — so nobody argues about whether it is current.
  • Permissions — not everyone should see every figure.

The most common failure here is not a missing chart, it's twelve charts nobody reads. Ship four and add the fifth when someone actually asks for it.

Yes, and this is usually the whole point. You can set the app to pull fresh data on a schedule — hourly, nightly, every Monday morning — so the dashboard is current before anyone opens it. Scheduled tasks cover how that runs. You can also trigger an alert when a number crosses a threshold, which is often more useful than the chart, because it reaches people who were never going to log in.

Choose on the decision the dashboard is meant to support:

  • The question — what will someone do differently after looking at it?
  • Your data shape — transactions, events, records or time series?
  • Refresh need — live, daily or monthly? Live costs more to keep honest.
  • Audience — an operator's screen and a board slide are different products.
  • Sources — check the ones you rely on can actually be connected.

Clone the closest match and point it at real data before judging the layout — most dashboards look fine with sample figures and fall apart on the real distribution. If none fits, describe what you need to the business intelligence app builder and Base44 builds the dashboard for you, with your sources connected and the charts and filters you asked for. Then keep refining it in plain language.
