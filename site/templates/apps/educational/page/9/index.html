Frequently asked questions

Educational app templates are pre-built teaching apps you copy and fill with your own material. The content, learners, submissions and marking arrive already connected. Unlike a worksheet, the work is recorded against the student, marking runs the moment they submit, and you can see which question the class got wrong rather than only who passed.

The parts that decide whether you'll use it a second time:

  • Mixed question types — multiple choice, short answer, ordering, image-based.
  • Automatic marking — with manual override for written answers.
  • A per-question view — what the class missed, not just who passed.
  • Attempt rules — one go, best of three, or open practice.
  • A shareable link — no student account setup if you can avoid it.
  • Export — results out to your own records or gradebook.

Pick on what you need to happen after a student finishes:

  • Purpose — practice, assessment, revision or a full course?
  • Marking — is everything closed-answer, or do you need to read responses?
  • Cohort — one class, a whole year group, or the public?
  • Repeat use — a one-off quiz and a reusable question bank differ a lot.
  • Records — does anything need to reach an official gradebook?
