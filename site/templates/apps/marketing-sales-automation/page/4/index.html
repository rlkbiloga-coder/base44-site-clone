Frequently asked questions

Marketing app templates are pre-built apps for running campaigns and pipelines — leads, deals, stages, owners and follow-ups, already structured. You copy one and put your own deals in rather than designing the pipeline first. What makes them more than a board is what happens between stages: the app can prompt the owner, send the follow-up and flag the deal that has not moved in two weeks.

They overlap, and which you want depends on where your problem is. A CRM is organized around the customer — every contact, every conversation, the whole relationship over years. A sales and marketing automation app is organized around the motion: campaigns going out, leads coming in, sequences firing, deals moving. If your problem is that you can't remember what you promised a customer in March, start from the CRM templates. If your problem is that leads arrive and nothing happens to them, start here.

The pieces that turn attention into revenue:

  • Lead capture — a form on your site that writes straight into the app.
  • Source tracking — which campaign, ad or page produced this person.
  • Stages with owners — and a rule for what happens when nothing moves.
  • Sequences — follow-ups that go out without anyone remembering.
  • Segments — so the message matches who is reading it.
  • Revenue attribution — closed deals traced back to the source.

Attribution is the one most people leave until later and then can't backfill, because the source has to be captured at the moment the lead arrives. Set it up on day one even if you don't look at it for months.

The honest caveat is that multi-touch journeys resist simple attribution: first touch and last touch will disagree, and both are useful.

Choose on where your process currently leaks:

  • Volume — a handful of large deals or hundreds of small ones?
  • Cycle length — same-day purchases and six-month deals need different tracking.
  • Channels — inbound forms, outbound email, ads, or events?
  • Team — one person doing everything, or marketing handing off to sales?
  • Automation depth — a single follow-up, or branching sequences?

Clone the closest match and run real leads through it for a fortnight. If your motion doesn't look like any of them, describe it to the marketing app builder.
