Website Builder FAQs

A website builder is a tool for making a website without writing code, using ready-made building blocks instead of hand-built HTML and CSS. Some are drag-and-drop visual editors, while other website builders go a step further and generate the whole site for you from a description. With Base44 vibe coding you build by plain-language chat: describe the site you want, get a working version back, then refine it by asking for changes. Learn more about the features of an AI website builder.

The best website builder is the one that fits what you need today and can grow with you tomorrow. Compare these 5 areas before you choose:

  • Ease of use: How fast can you go from an idea to a published site? Look for page edits and design changes that need no code.
  • What you can build: A portfolio or landing page needs a few pages and a contact form. A business website may also need user accounts, searchable content, booking flows, dashboards or payments.
  • Design flexibility: Check your control over layouts, branding, mobile responsiveness and reusable elements. Templates should speed you up, not lock in how your site looks.
  • Built-in business tools: Confirm that hosting, domains, SEO settings, forms, data storage, analytics and integrations are included. The more a platform handles natively, the less you maintain.
  • Room to grow: Your first version may be simple, but your needs will change. Pick a builder that adds pages, users, data and workflows without a rebuild.

Base44 covers all 5 in one place. Describe what you need, get a full website, then add forms, data and integrations – no add-ons to stitch together.

You create a website easily by describing what you need in plain language – no code, no drag-and-drop and it takes only 5 simple steps:

  • Describe your website: Tell Base44 the business, the pages you need and the tone you want. A few sentences is enough to start. Write it like a brief to a person – say who the site is for and the one action visitors should take, and the first version lands much closer.
  • Generate the full site: Base44's AI website builder returns a complete, working website in minutes, with layout, pages and copy already in place. Click through it like a first-time visitor before editing anything, and note what you want changed – that list becomes your next prompts.
  • Iterate in AI chat: Ask for changes in plain language. Rework a section, sharpen the copy or adjust the design, and watch it update. Ask for one change at a time, like "shorten the hero" or "add an FAQ section", so you can review each update as it lands.
  • Add growth essentials: Layer in contact forms, signup flows and lead-gen pages so the site brings in real business. Give every page one clear call to action, so visitors always know their next step.
  • Publish and share: Go live on a real URL in one click, then connect a free custom domain on any paid plan. Share the link as soon as it's live – feedback from real visitors tells you what to improve next, faster than polishing in private.
